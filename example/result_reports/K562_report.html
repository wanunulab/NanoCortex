Literature context

BCR::ABL1 is a previously characterized fusion in K562. Salmon et al. used K562 as an e14a2-positive reference cell line. This contextual observation does not resolve the subtype or validate the junction in the present dataset.

Salmon et al. (2022). Impact of BCR::ABL1 transcript type on RT-qPCR amplification performance and molecular response to therapy. Leukemia 36, 1879–1886. DOI: 10.1038/s41375-022-01612-2.

Other computational candidates

The remaining 15 displayed groups are retained for transparency as unvalidated computational candidates. Read support alone does not distinguish genuine fusion transcripts from alignment ambiguity, library artifacts, readthrough transcription, or unresolved annotation.

No claims of novelty, oncogenic activity, clinical significance, specific alternative BCR::ABL1 rearrangements, or definitive artifacts are made for these entries. A known fusion involving one partner does not establish the reported partner combination.

For biological follow-up, candidate-specific read alignments and reference annotations should be examined; junctional RT-PCR with Sanger sequencing can provide orthogonal transcript-level evidence. No such validation results are presented here.
